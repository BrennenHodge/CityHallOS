The problem

Small towns do big-city work with a fraction of the staff.

One city clerk might answer the phone, process paperwork, manage licenses, prep council meetings and keep the records — all before lunch. The work isn't the problem. The way it reaches City Hall is.

01

Fragmented communication

Requests arrive by phone, email, Facebook and the front counter, with no single way to capture, track and resolve them.

02

The same questions, every day

Staff repeat office hours, trash days and permit rules, chase missing documents, and re-type information into other systems.

03

Limited staff capacity

Smaller cities can't justify a specialist for every function, so a few people carry the full administrative load.

04

Knowledge scattered everywhere

Answers live in ordinances, PDFs, meeting minutes, old websites — and in the heads of long-time employees.

05

Residents left wondering

Once a request is made, residents rarely know whether it was received, assigned or fixed — so they call again.

06

Software that's hard to adopt

Traditional government software means long configurations, painful migrations and training nobody has time for.

What CityHallOS AI never does

Some decisions belong to people. Full stop.

  • Approve or deny permits and licenses
  • Make legal or statutory determinations
  • Handle emergencies instead of 911
  • Publish official minutes or notices without approval
  • Release records without human review
  • Invent policy when the answer isn't in your documents

Data handling, retention, recording consent and procurement are reviewed against applicable federal, state and local rules before every deployment.

FAQ

Questions city leaders ask us.

Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

Does CityHallOS replace our staff?

No. It takes repetitive work off their plate — the same questions, missing paperwork, re-typing and follow-ups — so your team can spend time on the work that needs a person. Employees stay in charge of decisions.

Do we have to replace our current software?

No. CityHallOS sits on top of the systems you already use. We don't replace accounting, payroll, utility billing or tax collection — over time we integrate with them.

What happens when the AI doesn't know the answer?

It says so. CityHallOS only answers from documents your city has approved, cites its source, and hands anything uncertain to a staff member with a summary, so nobody has to start over.

Can the AI approve permits or make legal decisions?

Never. The AI can explain requirements, check applications for completeness and prepare checklists, but statutory decisions always stay with your authorized officials.

What about emergencies?

Emergency and safety-critical messages immediately direct residents to 911 and the right emergency services. They never sit in an ordinary queue.

How is our data protected?

Each city's data is isolated from every other city's. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and every action is recorded in an audit log you can export for public records purposes.

How long does it take to get started?

Weeks, not months. A typical pilot spends the first couple of weeks measuring your baseline and loading your documents, then goes live on your website.

Who is CityHallOS built for?

Cities and towns of roughly 2,000 to 25,000 residents with small administrative teams, meaningful resident service volume and limited in-house technical resources. We're starting in Mississippi and expanding regionally.
